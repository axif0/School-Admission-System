| **F1** | No authentication; identity is trusted from `X-Student-Id` header | **PARTIAL** | `StudentIdValidation.cs:11-28`, `HoldEndpoints.cs:22-61` | **Do NOT implement JWT/OTP.** The specification explicitly mandates "No login" and header-based student identity.
